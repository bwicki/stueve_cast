StueveCast — Model Sounding Diagram

StueveCast draws Stüve, Emagram and Skew-T diagrams from numerical weather model profiles for any place and forecast hour, using the diagram and the fourteen analytics fields of the S2 Radiosonde Sounding Diagram tool. Pick a place on the map (or search, or use your position), pick an hour, pick one or more models. The first checked model is the primary one and drives the analytics; the others are drawn as dashed comparison curves.

Reading the chart: dots mark the real model levels; everything in between is interpolated. The green line is the model ground, which can differ from the real terrain. Drag the amber handle between the temperature plot and the wind-speed panel to give either one more room (double-tap resets). On a touch screen press and hold on the chart to read values, or lock the ✛ mode; pinch to zoom the altitude band, or use the two sliders on the left. On phones the profile opens zoomed to the lower part of the column (about the lowest 10 km); ⟲ shows the full column.

Time: the slider covers every hour the selected models provide (up to 16 days for the global models). Curves of a model disappear when the hour lies beyond its horizon. Model run times are estimated from each model's cycle.

Offline: the app shell and the last fetched profiles are cached, so the slider keeps working without a connection.

Data: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0) with pressure-level output of DWD ICON, Météo-France AROME/ARPEGE, NOAA GFS/HRRR, ECMWF IFS/AIFS, UK Met Office, ECCC GEM and JMA. MeteoSwiss ICON-CH1/CH2 have no pressure levels on Open-Meteo yet and are planned for a later version. Map tiles by OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap, place search by Open-Meteo geocoding, place names by Nominatim. Includes Leaflet (BSD-2-Clause) and qrcodejs (MIT).
